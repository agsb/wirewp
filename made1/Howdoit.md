
## Alternative Simplified Algorithm: 

### Force-Directed Placement

If you want something easier to code from scratch, use the Force-Directed (Spring-Embedder) approach:

    Treat every wire connection between chips as an attractive spring force (F = k ⋅ x).
    Treat the chips themselves as repulsive magnets so they do not overlap.
    Let the system naturally shift the chips around until the forces balance out. 

    Hook connections will pull heavily connected chips together, while the repulsive force keeps them spaced out cleanly across your board.
